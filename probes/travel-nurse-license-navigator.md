**Status: DRAFT. Nothing here is live.** Model drafts; operator ships (D3/D4).
**Inbound only — no cold outreach** (doctrine #7). See `BACKLOG.md` →

## What the probe tests
One question: **will a travel nurse give an email for a tool that tells them
exactly which license/compact they need for a target state before they sign a
contract?** Note the weak leg: reach is `diffuse` — this probe is really testing
whether *one community post converts*. If it doesn't, the diffuse-reach risk is
confirmed and the idea reshapes or dies.

## Pass bar (SET — do not move)
**≥10 self-serve sign-ups** from one travel-nurse community post, ~one week.
Below that = no signal → reshape or kill, log, move.
