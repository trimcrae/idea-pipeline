## What the probe tests
One question: **will a licensed tradesperson give an email for a single-state,
one-trade renewal/CE checker + reminder?** Their license is their livelihood and
they already search "[state] [trade] license renewal CE requirements" — inbound.

## Pick ONE trade + ONE state for v1
Lead with **[pest-control]** in one state (placeholder below). Pick the state
whose board PDF is the most painful to parse.

## Pass bar (SET — do not move)
**≥10 self-serve sign-ups** from search-intent traffic and/or one trade-forum
post, ~one week. Below that = no signal → reshape, log, move.

Rules change and are per-state (`maint:manual`); some boards publish this already
(`crowd:med`). The wedge is aggregation + plain-language + reminders, **one trade
at a time**. If it passes, the same engine ports to travel-nurse-license-navigator/cottage-food-permit-navigator/visa-bulletin-tracker/fleet-compliance-tracker/str-rule-navigator.
