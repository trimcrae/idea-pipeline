# Probe kit — F-01: Carrier fraud-risk checker for small freight brokers/dispatchers

**Status: DRAFT. Nothing here is live.** This is the test asset the model
drafts; the operator ships it (doctrine #4, STRATEGY.md D3/D4). Do not deploy
until you've decided to probe F-01 and set the pass bar.

Why F-01 first: it's the only `demand:proven` idea in the backlog (freight
fraud up ~1,500% since 2021; survey respondents losing >$200k in six months;
vetting is manual today). See `BACKLOG.md` → F-01.

## What the probe is testing

One question: **will a small dispatch shop / owner-operator give you their email
(or reply "yes") for a one-click carrier fraud check?** Not "is the idea good."
Not compliments. Action only.

## Pass bar (set this BEFORE shipping; don't move it)

- Cold outreach: **≥3 of 20** named recipients reply "yes, I'd use/pay."
- OR landing page: **≥10 sign-ups** from a single honest community post.
Anything less = no signal → kill or reshape, log it, move on.

## Asset A — landing page copy (single "notify me" button, no build of the product)

> **Headline:** Check a carrier for fraud before you book the load.
>
> **Sub:** Paste an MC number or a broker email. Get an instant risk flag —
> authority age, lapsed insurance, lookalike-domain clones, and one-phone-
> across-many-authorities clustering. Built for one-truck operators and small
> dispatch shops, not enterprise brokerages.
>
> **The one thing it does:** the 30-second check you currently do by hand across
> SAFER, a phone call, and a gut feeling — in one click.
>
> **CTA button:** Notify me when it's live  · [email field]
>
> **Honest footer:** Early. No product yet — I'm checking if small shops want
> this before building it.

## Asset B — cold DM / email template (send to 10–20 named small shops)

> Subject: the manual carrier vetting you do before booking
>

## Where to ship it (operator picks the lightest)

- Cold DMs/emails to named small dispatch shops and owner-operators (best —
  enumerable, and the pain is concrete).
- One honest post in an owner-operator / dispatch subreddit or Facebook group.

  form. **No product behind it. No ad spend unless you choose to.**
