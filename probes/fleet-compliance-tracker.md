# Probe kit — fleet-compliance-tracker: DOT/FMCSA deadline calendar for tiny trucking operations

**Status: DRAFT. Nothing here is live.** The model drafts the asset; the operator
ships it (doctrine #4, D3/D4). **Inbound only — no cold outreach** (#7). **$0 — no
paid ads, organic traffic only** (#9/D11). Set the pass bar (below) before running.

Why this one: it's the `trade-license-renewal-navigator` compliance-navigator
engine pointed at trucking, and it shares an inbound audience with
`freight-fraud-check` (the only `demand:proven` idea) — owner-operators and tiny
dispatch shops who already search "MCS-150 due," "UCR renewal," "DOT compliance
deadlines." See `BACKLOG.md` → fleet-compliance-tracker.

## What the probe is testing

One question: **will a 1–5 truck owner-operator who found this themselves give an
email to be reminded before their MCS-150 / UCR / IFTA / CDL-medical deadlines?**
Not "is it useful." Self-serve action only.

## Pass bar (SET — do not move)

**≥10 self-serve sign-ups from one low-effort, $0 inbound push** (a single
community post or an SEO-able free checker page — no paid ads, D11), within about
a week. Below that = no signal → kill or reshape, log it, move on. No
replies-in-principle — only sign-ups count.

## Asset A — landing page (single "remind me" button; no product behind it)

> **Headline:** Never miss a DOT deadline that shuts your truck down.
>
> **Sub:** Enter your DOT number. Get one calendar of the dates a small operation
> can't miss — MCS-150 biennial update, UCR, IFTA quarterly, CDL medical card,
> drug-&-alcohol program, annual inspection — and an email before each is due.
>
> **The one thing it does:** the deadline-tracking a big fleet's TMS does, for the
> one-to-five-truck operator who tracks it on a wall calendar.
>
> **CTA button:** Remind me before my deadlines  · [email field]
>
> **Honest footer:** Early — no product yet. We surface the published .gov
> deadline and link; we don't tell you you're compliant. Checking if small
> operators want this before building it.

## Asset B — one broadcast community post (post once; don't work the thread)

Drop a single honest post where owner-operators/dispatchers gather (an
owner-operator subreddit or a dispatch Facebook group that allows it), linking
the page. One post, then watch sign-ups — not a DM campaign.

> Title: A dead-simple "when's my MCS-150 / UCR / IFTA due" reminder — would you use it?
>
> Body: Big fleets get deadline tracking from their TMS. The rest of us track
> MCS-150, UCR, IFTA, CDL medical, and the drug-&-alcohol stuff on a wall
> calendar — and a missed MCS-150 deactivates your authority. I'm checking if a
> dead-simple version is worth building: enter your DOT#, get one calendar +
> email reminders. Not built yet, nothing to buy. Link if you want to be
> notified: [page]
