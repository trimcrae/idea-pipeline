# Probe kit — paralegal-filing-validator: per-jurisdiction court-filing rules validator

**Status: DRAFT. Nothing here is live.** Model drafts; operator ships (D3/D4).
**Inbound only — no cold outreach** (doctrine #7). See `BACKLOG.md` →
`paralegal-filing-validator`. Cluster sibling of `trade-license-renewal-navigator`
/ `travel-nurse-license-navigator` / `cottage-food-permit-navigator` /
`visa-bulletin-tracker` — same engine, different niche.

## What the probe tests
One question: **will a freelance paralegal give an email for a one-jurisdiction
filing-rules checklist/validator?** A rejected filing or blown deadline is
malpractice exposure for the attorney — real, expensive pain.

## Pick ONE filing type + ONE court for v1
Start with a single high-volume filing type in one busy jurisdiction (the
placeholder below is **[civil complaint]** in **[county/court]**).

## Pass bar (SET — do not move)
**≥10 self-serve sign-ups** from one paralegal community post, ~one week. Below
that = no signal → reshape or kill, log, move.

## Asset A — landing page (one button; no product behind it)
> **Headline:** Filing a [civil complaint] in [County] court? Check your format,
> service, and deadline rules before the clerk rejects it.
>
> **Sub:** A current checklist/validator for that one court's formatting, service
> requirements, and deadlines — so a refile or a missed rule doesn't cost your
> attorney a deadline.
>
> **The one thing it does:** turns the private checklist you keep in your head
> into a current, court-specific validator.
>
> **CTA:** Get the [County] filing checklist · [email field]
>
> **Honest footer:** Early. Starting with one filing type + court to confirm it's
> wanted before expanding.

## Asset B — one broadcast community post (post once; don't work the thread)
A paralegal subreddit/forum/FB group that allows it.
> Title: A per-court "did I get the filing rules right?" validator — would you use it?
>
> Body: Rejected filings and missed service/format rules are a recurring headache,
> and the rules differ by court. Thinking about a one-jurisdiction checklist kept
> current. Not built yet. If it covered [County] [filing type], want it? [page]

## Asset C — tiny ad (optional)
Lower priority — paralegal communities are the natural channel.

## Honest note / catch
Rules vary by court and change — staying current is real upkeep
(`maint:manual`); start with one region. Audience is searchable but not tightly
enumerable. The wedge is the narrow, current, court-specific answer.
