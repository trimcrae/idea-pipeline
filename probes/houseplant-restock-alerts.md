# Probe kit — houseplant-restock-alerts: Rare-houseplant restock alerts

**Status: DRAFT. Nothing here is live.** Model drafts; operator ships (D3/D4).
**Inbound only — no cold outreach** (doctrine #7). See `BACKLOG.md` → houseplant-restock-alerts.

## What the probe tests
One question: **will a collector give an email/number to be alerted the moment
one specific cultivar restocks across the few shops that carry it?** Restock
alerting is a proven-pattern behavior; this tests it in an uncrowded niche.

## Pass bar (SET — do not move)
**≥10 self-serve sign-ups** for a single cultivar's alert, from one community
post, ~one week. Below that = no signal → kill or reshape, log, move on.

## Asset A — landing page (one "notify me" button; no product behind it)
> **Headline:** Get a text the second [Variegated Monstera] is back in stock.
>
> **Sub:** We watch the handful of shops that actually carry it and ping you the
> moment it drops — so you're not refreshing 20 tabs or missing it by minutes.
>
> **The one thing it does:** one alert, one cultivar, across the 5 shops worth
> watching. No feeds, no accounts to babysit.
>
> **CTA:** Alert me when it restocks · [email / phone field]
>
> **Honest footer:** Early. Starting with one cultivar to see if it's wanted.

## Asset B — one broadcast community post (post once; don't work the thread)
Where rare-plant collectors gather (a houseplant subreddit or buy/sell/trade
group that allows it).
> Title: A restock alert for [cultivar] across the shops that carry it — useful?
>
> Body: These sell out in minutes and I'm tired of refreshing. Thinking about a
> free alert: pick a cultivar, get pinged the second any of ~5 shops restocks it.
> Not built yet. If you'd want it for [cultivar], drop your email: [page]

## Asset C — tiny ad (optional)
Low priority — community post is the natural inbound channel here.

## Honest note / catch
Scrapers break when shops change their pages — that's agent-absorbable upkeep,
but you must seed the watch-list of which shops to monitor. Pick the single
most-wanted cultivar and its real shop list before shipping.
