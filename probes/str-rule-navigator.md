# Probe kit — str-rule-navigator: short-term-rental host permit & lodging-tax-rule navigator

**Status: DRAFT. Nothing here is live.** The model drafts the asset; the operator
ships it (doctrine #4, D3/D4). **Inbound only — no cold outreach** (#7). **$0 — no
paid ads, organic traffic only** (#9/D11). Set the pass bar (below) before running.

Why this one: the compliance-navigator engine pointed at Airbnb/STR hosts, who
have revenue, face fines/delisting for operating unpermitted, and search
"[city] short-term rental rules / permit / registration / lodging tax"
constantly. See `BACKLOG.md` → str-rule-navigator. Pick ONE metro for v1 (the
rules are hyper-local).

## What the probe is testing

One question: **will a host in [chosen city] who found this themselves give an
email for a plain-language "what does this city require of me + when are the
deadlines" answer?** Not compliments — self-serve action only.

## Pass bar (SET — do not move)

**≥10 self-serve sign-ups from one low-effort, $0 inbound push** (a single
community post or an SEO-able free checker page — no paid ads, D11), within about
a week. Below that = no signal → kill or reshape, log it, move on.

## Asset A — landing page (single "check my city" / "notify me" button)

> **Headline:** Don't get fined for an unpermitted short-term rental in [City].
>
> **Sub:** One plain-language answer: do you need an STR permit or registration
> number here, do you owe transient-occupancy/lodging tax (and how often do you
> file), are there night caps or primary-residence rules — plus the renewal and
> tax deadlines, with a reminder before each.
>
> **The one thing it does:** turns [City]'s STR ordinance and tax pages into a
> checklist + deadlines, instead of you reading the municipal code.
>
> **CTA button:** Check [City]'s rules / notify me  · [email field]
>
> **Honest footer:** Early — no product yet. We summarize the published ordinance
> and link the source; we don't tell you you're legal to operate — verify with
> the city. Checking if hosts want this before building it.

Drop a single honest post in an STR/Airbnb-host community for that metro (a
city-specific host Facebook group or an STR subreddit), linking the page.

> Title: A plain-language "what does [City] require of STR hosts + when are the deadlines" tool — useful?
>
> Body: [City] keeps tightening short-term-rental rules — permit/registration,
