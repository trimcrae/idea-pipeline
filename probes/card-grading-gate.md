# Probe kit — card-grading-gate: "Should I grade this card?" expected-value gate

**Status: DRAFT. Nothing here is live.** Model drafts; operator ships (D3/D4).
**Inbound only — no cold outreach** (doctrine #7). See `BACKLOG.md` → card-grading-gate.

## What the probe tests
One question: **will a collector who found this themselves give an email for a
pre-submission "is grading worth it" calculator?** Not compliments — action.

## Pass bar (SET — do not move)
**≥10 self-serve sign-ups** from one community post and/or a ~$20 ad, ~one week.
Below that = no signal → kill or reshape, log, move on.

exist. The defensible slice is the **narrow pre-submission decision**, not
another price database. If the probe passes, that scope discipline is the build.
