# Probe kit — estate-lowball-gate: "Am I being lowballed?" independent value-range gate for estate liquidation

**Status: DRAFT. Nothing here is live.** Model drafts; operator ships (D3/D4).
**Inbound only — no cold outreach** (doctrine #7). See `BACKLOG.md` → estate-lowball-gate.

## What the probe tests
One question: **will an heir/executor about to accept an estate-buyer's offer
give an email for an independent value range first?** They already search "how
much is my late parent's [X] collection worth" — this tests whether that
distrust-driven intent converts before the (one-time) transaction.

## Pick ONE category for v1
Lead with the category that has the cleanest public sold-comp data — likely
**[coins]** or vinyl records (deep eBay/auction sold history). The page below
uses **[coin]** as the placeholder.

## Pass bar (SET — do not move)
**≥10 self-serve sign-ups or pre-orders** from search-intent traffic, ~one week.
Below that = no signal → reshape, log, move. (See catch: low repeat means this
must be cheap and SEO-fed; the probe is the cheapest test of that.)

## Asset A — landing page (one button; no product behind it)
> **Headline:** Don't let the estate buyer lowball your late parent's [coin]
> collection.
>
> **Sub:** Before you accept an offer or let a company haul it away, get an
> independent value *range* from recent sold prices — so you know if you're
> being fair-dealt or fleeced.
>
> **The one thing it does:** a defensible second opinion on what the [coins] are
> actually worth, in plain numbers, from someone not trying to buy them.
>
> **CTA:** Get an independent value range · [email field for early access]
>
> **Honest footer:** Early. No product yet — confirming families want this
> before building it. Not a formal appraisal.
>
> **Trust note (matches the wedge):** We don't buy collections. We're not the
> estate company. The whole point is an opinion from someone with nothing to gain.

## Asset B — one broadcast community post (post once)
Where executors/heirs or [coin] collectors gather (a probate/estate-settlement
or collecting subreddit/forum that allows it). Frame it as helping families, not
selling.

>
> Body: When you're settling an estate, the buyer is also the appraiser, and
> heirs have no idea if they're being fair-dealt. Thinking about a simple tool:

## Asset C — tiny ad (recommended here)
Purest test: target "how much is my [coin] collection worth / inherited [coin]
collection value / estate [coin] appraisal" intent; measure click → sign-up.

## Honest note / catch
**One-time use per customer (low LTV)** — there's no retention, so it lives or
dies on cheap searchable acquisition; that's exactly what the ad-driven probe
tests. Valuation borders on judgment (`maint:manual`); keep v1 to one category
with clean sold-comp data and ship a *range*, not a false-precision number.
