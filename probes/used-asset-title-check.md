# Probe kit — used-asset-title-check: Pre-purchase title/lien/theft check for used big-ticket non-car assets

**Status: DRAFT. Nothing here is live.** Model drafts; operator ships (D3/D4).
**Inbound only — no cold outreach** (doctrine #7). See `BACKLOG.md` → used-asset-title-check.

## What the probe tests
One question: **will someone about to buy a used $10k+ trailer/RV/boat/ATV pay
for (or pre-order) a lien/theft/salvage check the way car buyers pay for Carfax?**
Pre-purchase history checks are a proven paid behavior for cars; this tests
whether the intent transfers to the asset classes with no Carfax.

## Pick ONE asset class for v1
Lead with the class that has the cleanest public coverage (NMVTIS reaches many
titled trailers/RVs/powersports; boats/equipment are more fragmented). The page
below uses **[trailer]** as the placeholder — swap to whichever class you ship.

## Pass bar (SET — do not move)
**≥10 self-serve sign-ups or pre-orders** from search-intent traffic (a small ad
and/or one community post), ~one week. Below that = no signal → reshape, log, move.

## Asset A — landing page (one button; no product behind it)
> **Headline:** Buying a used [trailer]? Check it for liens, theft, and salvage
> before you hand over $10k.
>
> **Sub:** Enter the VIN/serial and get the history a car buyer takes for
> granted — outstanding liens, theft/salvage flags, title issues — for the
> assets that don't have a Carfax.
>
> **The one thing it does:** the 60-second "is this safe to buy" check, for
> [trailers] specifically.
>
> **CTA:** Check before you buy · [email field for early access / pre-order]
>
> **Honest footer:** Early. No product yet — confirming buyers want this for
> [trailers] before building it.

## Asset B — one broadcast community post (post once)
Where used-[trailer] buyers gather (a marketplace/owner subreddit or FB group).

>
> Body: Cars have Carfax; [trailers] don't, and a stolen or lien-encumbered one

## Asset C — tiny ad (recommended here)
This idea is purest as a search-intent test: target "[trailer] VIN check / lien
check / is it stolen" and measure click → sign-up.

## Honest note / catch
Coverage is patchy by class and state — v1 lives or dies on picking the class
with the best public data. Cars are crowded; the wedge is the non-car asset
nobody serves. `maint:manual` until coverage is mapped.
