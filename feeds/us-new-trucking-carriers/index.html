Rows are FMCSA public records as published (registration, licensing and insurance filings). A filing is not a finding of fraud, non-compliance or bad faith.

Questions

Is this the same as a 'new MC authority' list?

It is earlier: every new USDOT registration (for-hire carriers, private fleets, brokers) in the week it was added to the FMCSA census. Authority grants follow days to weeks later — see the separate MC-authority feed.

Do rows include phone and email?

Yes — the phone and email the company filed with FMCSA, exactly as published in the public census. The free sample masks them; the paid CSV does not.

How is the file delivered?

A download page, refreshed every Monday morning (UTC) with the previous 7 days. The last 8 weeks stay available. CSV, UTF-8, one header row.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the link in your Stripe receipt. No minimum term.

Where does the data come from?

FMCSA Motor Carrier Census (US DOT open data, updated daily). It is pulled from the agency's public open-data portal every week, filtered to the rows that are new, and normalised into one flat table. Source: https://data.transportation.gov/Trucking-and-Motorcoaches/Company-Census-File/az4n-8mr2
