Questions

Why no owner or applicant names?

By design: only business names are included. Owners who filed as private individuals appear with a blank owner_business column.

How is the file delivered?

A download page, refreshed every Monday morning (UTC) with the previous 7 days. The last 8 weeks stay available. CSV, UTF-8, one header row.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the link in your Stripe receipt. No minimum term.

Where does the data come from?

NYC Department of Buildings — DOB NOW: Build, Approved Permits (NYC Open Data, updated daily). It is pulled from the agency's public open-data portal every week, filtered to the rows that are new, and normalised into one flat table. Source: https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Housing-Development/DOB-NOW-Build-Approved-Permits/rbx6-tga4
