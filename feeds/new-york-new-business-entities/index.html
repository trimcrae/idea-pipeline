Questions

How is the file delivered?

A download page, refreshed every Monday morning (UTC) with the previous 7 days. The last 8 weeks stay available. CSV, UTF-8, one header row.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the link in your Stripe receipt. No minimum term.

Where does the data come from?

NY Department of State — Active Corporations (data.ny.gov, updated daily). It is pulled from the agency's public open-data portal every week, filtered to the rows that are new, and normalised into one flat table. Source: https://data.ny.gov/Economic-Development/Active-Corporations-Beginning-1800/n9v6-gdp6
