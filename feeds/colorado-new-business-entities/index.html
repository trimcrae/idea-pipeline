Questions

How is the file delivered?

A download page, refreshed every Monday morning (UTC) with the previous 7 days. The last 8 weeks stay available. CSV, UTF-8, one header row.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the link in your Stripe receipt. No minimum term.

Where does the data come from?

Colorado Secretary of State — Business Entities (data.colorado.gov, updated daily). It is pulled from the agency's public open-data portal every week, filtered to the rows that are new, and normalised into one flat table. Source: https://data.colorado.gov/Business/Business-Entities-in-Colorado/4ykn-tg5h
