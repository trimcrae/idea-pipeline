Trucking insurance cancellation notices filed with FMCSA this week

Every BMC-91/34 insurance cancellation an insurer filed in the past 7 days — carrier, docket, policy, insurer, cancellation-effective date — joined to the carrier's name, phone and email. Weekly CSV.

Who buys thisTruck-insurance agents and MGAs: a cancellation notice means the carrier has ~30 days to replace its liability or cargo policy or lose its authority. Brokers use the same list as a risk watchlist.
Why it worksInsurers must notify FMCSA before cancelling; FMCSA publishes the filing. It is the earliest public signal that a carrier is shopping for insurance.

Questions

How is the file delivered?

A download page, refreshed every Monday morning (UTC) with the previous 7 days. The last 8 weeks stay available. CSV, UTF-8, one header row.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the link in your Stripe receipt. No minimum term.

Where does the data come from?

FMCSA Licensing & Insurance — Active & Pending Insurance, Carrier and Census files (US DOT open data). It is pulled from the agency's public open-data portal every week, filtered to the rows that are new, and normalised into one flat table.
