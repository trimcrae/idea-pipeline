Questions

How is the file delivered?

A download page, refreshed every Monday morning (UTC) with the previous 7 days. The last 8 weeks stay available. CSV, UTF-8, one header row.

Can I cancel?

Where does the data come from?

Texas Alcoholic Beverage Commission — Pending Original License Applications (data.texas.gov, updated daily). It is pulled from the agency's public open-data portal every week, filtered to the rows that are new, and normalised into one flat table. Source: https://data.texas.gov/dataset/Pending-Original-New-Primary-and-Subordinate-Licen/mxm5-tdpj
