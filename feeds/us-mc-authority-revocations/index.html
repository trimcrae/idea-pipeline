How is the file delivered?

A download page, refreshed every Monday morning (UTC) with the previous 7 days. The last 8 weeks stay available. CSV, UTF-8, one header row.

Where does the data come from?

FMCSA Licensing & Insurance — Authority History, Carrier and Census files (US DOT open data). It is pulled from the agency's public open-data portal every week, filtered to the rows that are new, and normalised into one flat table.
